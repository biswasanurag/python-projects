Arches National Park
    Description: Desert park outside Moab, with over 2,000 sandstone arches.
    State(s): Utah
    Entrance fee: $30
    Best Seasons: spring, fall
    Activities: hiking, climbing, stargazing, scenic driving

Canyonlands National Park
    Description: Desert of canyons and mesas split by the Green and Colorado rivers into four districts.
    State(s): Utah
    Entrance fee: $30
    Best Seasons: spring, fall
    Activities: hiking, paddling, climbing, stargazing, scenic driving

Capitol Reef National Park
    Description: Long ridge of rock called the Waterpocket Fold, with historic fruit orchards.
    State(s): Utah
    Entrance fee: $20
    Best Seasons: spring, fall
    Activities: hiking, climbing, scenic driving, stargazing

Zion National Park
    Description: Sandstone canyon cut by the Virgin River; shuttle access in peak season.
    State(s): Utah
    Entrance fee: $35
    Best Seasons: spring, fall
    Activities: hiking, climbing, canyoneering, stargazing
